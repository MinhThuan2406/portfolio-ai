# Design Acknowledgement & Credits

This portfolio's visual concept, layout rhythm, and interactive direction are deeply inspired by the work of **Thomas Sarazin** and **Nualt Studio** on **[Nualt](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/nualt)**, recognized as an **Awwwards Nominee**.

---

### Special Thanks & Appreciation

A heartfelt thank you to the creators of [Nualt](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/nualt) for pioneering such a distinctive and inspiring technical design language:

- **Color Palette & Atmosphere**: The refined pairing of ivory drafting paper (`#f4f2e8`), deep cobalt ink (`#0755b5`), and verification green accents.
- **Blueprint Precision**: The mathematical multi-column grid, 1px hairline framing, and sharp geometric corners.
- **Editorial Typography**: The purposeful dialogue between high-contrast headings and technical monospace annotations.
- **Living Systems**: The concept of treating digital interfaces and diagrams as empirical engineering dossiers.
